Training transcript tips

Listen to the audio and fix the text to match what was actually said. ALL CAPS is fine — the transcript collector converts everything to lowercase for training. Approve & Send stays off if the clip is over 30 seconds, the text is only a no-voice or tones label, a street abbreviation is still short, or a number is written as a word.

  • 30 seconds or less — longer calls cannot be sent. Whisper trains on 30-second windows, so a longer clip would no longer match the transcript.
  • Spoken words only — do not send a clip with no voice, or only tones. A label such as “no voice” or “tones only” cannot be sent.
  • Numbers as digits — addresses, units, plates, times, and codes. Number words cannot be sent. forty two main street → 42 main street
  • Streets & places — spell out street types and directions. broadway & magnolia ave → broadway and magnolia avenue 410 n 8th ave → 410 north 8th avenue
  • Highways & routes — use digits for route numbers, and spell out route. interstate seventy one north → interstate 71 north rt three fifteen → route 315
  • Sound-alike words — pick the word that fits dispatch context. white mail suspect → white male suspect two units two scene → 2 units to scene en route two the seen → en route to the scene red break lights → red brake lights
  • Unit & radio IDs — use digits when spoken as numbers. engine twelve → engine 12
  • License plates — letters and digits exactly as read on the air. plate oh four seven kay en → plate oh 047 kn Keep “oh” when it is the letter O.
  • 10-codes & signals — match how they're spoken on your system. ten fifty → 10-50
  • Medical & fire terms — spell out what was said; don't abbreviate unless they did. gsw to the leg → gunshot wound to the leg
  • Only what's spoken — remove tones, beeps, or hallucinated filler; don't add words that aren't in the audio.
